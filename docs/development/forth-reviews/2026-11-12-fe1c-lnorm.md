## 3. Findings
- **L1 (marks API).** A rollback after a commit is neither refused nor harmless while an outer mark is open: marks are
  bare trail positions with one shared count. Probe: open outer, assign a, open inner, assign b, commit inner, roll
  back inner again: the stale rollback undoes b, closes the last count and clears the trail, so the outer rollback no
  longer undoes a. A double rollback or any out-of-order use does the same. Today's one caller (ld-close) is strictly
  LIFO and once, so nothing is wrong now; FE1e will nest more. Fix: a mark carrying its depth (or a two-cell mark:
  trail position, depth); rollback and commit refuse unless the mark's depth equals the current count; plant a fault.
- **L2 (exceptions).** ld-loop is not under CATCH: a THROW inside it would leave the mark open and the assignments
  made, where Lean's checkpointDefEq restores on an exception. Fix: `['] ld-loop catch`, roll back on a nonzero code,
  rethrow, as elab-read does.
- **L3 (class 5).** The trail refusing a note (refusing the assignment or delay) and ld-pairs' full branches are never
  run; meta-heap.fs's per-allocation refusal can reach both.
- **I1.** Normalize costs about n² comparisons and n²/2 new levels for a max of n arguments; documented, on the plan.
- **I2.** Numeric name parts compare by `-` and a sign: exact while parts stay below 2^31 on 32-bit cells; `u<` and `=`
  would be exact for every value.
