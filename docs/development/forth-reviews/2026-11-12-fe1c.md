## 2. Loose bound variables (Q2)
- instantiate1: index == depth becomes the value; a loose index > depth becomes index - 1; index < depth stays (kept through the range test). A value with loose variables answers unsupported, where Lean would lift it.
- The depth is +1 only for a lambda's or Pi's body (lf-term-binder-tag?).
- A let is LET(v, fun x : T => b) (terms.fs:121-126): its value and lambda are at the node's depth, and b is under the lambda's binder. That is Lean's letE (type and value at depth, body at depth + 1).
- A projection's children are at the node's depth.
- abstract: xi becomes #(depth + n - 1 - i); the scan from the end makes the last match win, as Lean's loop does; a node without fvar or mvar is skipped (data word known, no fvar flag); metavariables are not instantiated.
- headBeta: the lambdas are counted on f (betaRev's go). For closed arguments, sequential instantiate1 equals Lean's instantiate of them all at once. The leftover arguments are applied in order; zero arguments give f.
