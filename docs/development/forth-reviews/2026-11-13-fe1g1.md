- **I1, the current view:** `lf-mvar-new` records the current view (`lf-mctx-view`), empty after a reset. Callers that
  introduce free variables (FE1e's elaborator, FE1g-3's `intro`) must push them onto the current view; infer.fs's
  telescopes do not (harmless today: infer makes no metavariables). If a caller forgets, the "may" dependency misses
  and `clear` could remove a variable Lean would refuse to remove — a refused term later, never an accepted one. A
  sentence in mvars.fs's header and a test once FE1g-3 adds `intro`.
- **I2, grouping lets and haves:** the printer's grouping comparison treats a let and a have as different where Lean's
  `==` ignores nonDep; a printer gap, stated in the code.
- **I3, merge note:** tests/front/whnf.fs adds the have tests to fe1c-2's file; merge it after the fe1e1 merge, whose
  fixture is format 2.
